# 🛒 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

> **End-to-End Data Analytics Project using Python, SQL & Power BI**

An end-to-end customer behavior analytics project designed to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights.

The project analyzes **3,900 customer transactions across 18 attributes** to understand purchasing behavior, product performance, customer segments, subscription patterns, sales, and revenue trends.

The complete workflow covers **data cleaning with Python, business analysis using SQL, and interactive visualization using Power BI**.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of customer transaction data, but raw data alone does not provide meaningful business insights.

This project focuses on answering questions such as:

* Which product categories generate the highest sales and revenue?
* Which customer age groups contribute the most revenue?
* What proportion of customers are subscribed?
* How do customer demographics affect purchasing behavior?
* Which categories should receive greater marketing and inventory attention?
* How can customer behavior data support better business decisions?

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing patterns
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Compare sales and revenue across categories
* Analyze customer behavior by age group and gender
* Compare subscribed and non-subscribed customers
* Examine the influence of discounts and shipping methods
* Analyze customer ratings and purchasing behavior
* Generate actionable business recommendations

## 💡 Key Insights

### 🥇 Clothing Leads Sales

Clothing is the strongest-performing category in the dashboard, generating the highest sales volume and revenue.

### 🥈 Accessories Perform Strongly

Accessories represent the second-largest contributor to sales and revenue.

### 👥 Young Adults Are a Major Revenue Segment

The Young Adult customer group contributes the highest revenue and sales among the displayed age groups.

### 📱 Subscription Opportunity

The majority of customers are currently **non-subscribers**, indicating a potential opportunity for the business to improve subscription adoption through targeted offers and loyalty benefits.

### 📦 Category Performance

Footwear and Outerwear contribute less than Clothing and Accessories, suggesting an opportunity to investigate pricing, promotions, product demand, and inventory strategy.

---

## 📈 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies could be considered:

1. **Strengthen Clothing & Accessories**

   * Prioritize inventory and marketing for high-performing categories.

2. **Improve Subscription Conversion**

   * Introduce targeted discounts, loyalty benefits, and personalized offers for non-subscribers.

3. **Target High-Value Age Groups**

   * Develop personalized campaigns for customer segments contributing higher revenue.

4. **Optimize Lower-Performing Categories**

   * Analyze product-level performance in Footwear and Outerwear before allocating additional marketing budget.

5. **Use Customer Segmentation**

   * Create targeted campaigns based on demographics, purchasing frequency, subscription status, and spending behavior.
